🔨 Step 4 — Fully Defined Student Task

What to build

  • Add CSS transition or animation to option hover/selection.
  • Add correct/wrong visual effects.
  • Add optional sound placeholders or audio tags/files.
  • Play or trigger sound/visual feedback after answer check.
  • Ensure animations do not block gameplay.

Acceptance Criteria

  • Correct/wrong feedback feels clear.
  • Buttons have hover/press effect.
  • No animation makes text unreadable.
  • App still works without audio files.

What to submit to the teacher

  • Screenshot or short recording of animation.
  • Explain where animation code is written.

Do not move ahead until

  • The project runs without errors.
  • You can explain which files changed.
  • The acceptance criteria above are satisfied.
