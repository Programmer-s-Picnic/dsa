How to Play

1
Join the room

Enter your name, select a player colour and connect to the game room.

2
Start the game

At least two players are required. A computer joins automatically when only one human player is available.

3
Roll the dice

Click Roll Dice when it is your turn.

4
Select Power Points

Add Power Points when you want to move farther than the dice result.

5
Make your move

Check the final movement and click the green movement button.

🪜 Ladder Climb to a higher square.
🐍 Snake Fall to a lower square.
⚡ Power Points Every player starts with 6.
🏁 Winning Reach exactly 100 after using all Power Points.
