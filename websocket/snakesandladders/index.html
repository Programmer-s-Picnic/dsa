How to Play

1
Choose a free position

Positions already owned by another player are disabled.

2
Connect to the room

The game checks the room before confirming your position.

3
Start the game

A computer joins automatically when there is only one human.

4
Roll and select Power

Remaining Power Points are always shown above the board.

5
Reach exactly 100

All Power Points must be used before winning.

🪜 Ladder Climb upwards.
🐍 Snake Fall downwards.

🔒 Protected One connected owner per position.
